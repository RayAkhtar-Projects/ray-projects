Project Overview

This project analysed Rolls-Royce Holdings plc using financial information extracted from annual reports between 2020 and 2025. The objective was to evaluate the company's post-pandemic recovery, profitability, operational efficiency and future growth prospects. An interactive Excel dashboard was developed using financial KPIs, trend analysis, forecasting techniques and executive-level data visualisations to communicate business performance.

Key Findings

Strong Post-Pandemic Recovery

Following the severe downturn experienced during the pandemic, revenue growth accelerated as global air travel recovered and engine flying hours increased.

Profitability Improved Significantly

Operating profit increased substantially due to cost reductions, pricing improvements and transformation initiatives across the business.

ROCE Increased Year-on-Year

The company generated stronger returns from its capital base, indicating improved operational efficiency and resource utilisation.

Cash Generation Strengthened

Cash conversion exceeded historical levels, providing greater financial flexibility and supporting future investment opportunities.

Business Recommendations

Management Action Plan

The analysis suggests that Rolls-Royce should continue to focus on operational efficiency improvements while leveraging growth in the Civil Aerospace division. Management should maintain strong cash conversion and capital discipline while investing in future technologies, including sustainable aviation and advanced propulsion systems. Maintaining profitability and cash generation will be critical for sustaining long-term shareholder value.
