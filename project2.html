Project Overview

This project analysed Rolls-Royce Holdings plc using financial data extracted from annual reports covering 2020 to 2025. The objective was to evaluate the company's revenue growth, profitability, operational efficiency and overall financial recovery following the COVID-19 pandemic. An interactive Excel dashboard was developed to communicate key performance indicators, historical trends and future forecasts to support strategic decision-making.

Key Findings

Strong Post-Pandemic Recovery

Revenue growth accelerated significantly after 2021, supported by the recovery of global air travel and higher engine flying hours within the Civil Aerospace division.

Profitability Increased Significantly

Operational improvements, cost reductions and pricing initiatives contributed to a substantial increase in profitability across the business.

ROCE Improved Year-on-Year

Returns generated from invested capital strengthened considerably, indicating improved efficiency and stronger operational performance.

Cash Generation Became a Key Strength

Cash conversion improved substantially throughout the period, supporting liquidity, debt reduction and future investment opportunities.

Business Recommendations

Management Action Plan

The analysis suggests Rolls-Royce should continue its focus on operational efficiency, margin improvement and strong cash generation. Management should maintain investment in high-growth aerospace and defence markets while preserving capital discipline. Continued focus on cost control and technological innovation should support long-term shareholder value creation and sustainable growth.
