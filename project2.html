Rolls-Royce Financial Performance Dashboard

This project analyses Rolls-Royce Holdings plc using financial data extracted from annual reports between 2020 and 2025. The dashboard evaluates revenue growth, operating profit, ROCE, cash conversion, and future forecasts to assess the company's financial recovery, efficiency improvements and long-term outlook.

Project Overview

Objective

To evaluate Rolls-Royce's financial performance using annual report data and identify key trends in growth, profitability and operational efficiency.

Tools Used

Microsoft Excel, Pivot Tables, Power Query, Financial Modelling, Forecasting Techniques, Dashboard Design and KPI Analysis.

Skills Demonstrated

Financial Analysis, Business Intelligence, Forecasting, Data Visualisation, Ratio Analysis and Executive Reporting.

Key Insights

Strong Recovery

Following severe disruption caused by Covid-19, Rolls-Royce experienced substantial revenue growth, driven by increased civil aerospace flying hours and improved demand across its key business segments.

Improving Profitability

Operating performance improved significantly due to transformation initiatives, cost-control programmes and a greater focus on high-margin activity.

Excellent Cash Generation

Cash conversion improved year-on-year, strengthening liquidity and providing greater flexibility for future investment and shareholder returns.

Forecast Analysis

Revenue forecasting was performed using historical growth trends from 2020-2025. Based on current performance, Rolls-Royce is expected to continue generating revenue growth, supported by increasing engine flying hours, defence contracts and ongoing operational improvements.
